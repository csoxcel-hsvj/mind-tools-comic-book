# 🧠 Mind Tools Comic Book

> 23 thinking tools for ages 10 to 17, told through comics, true stories, games and activities

Learn how scientists, engineers and investors think. Meet **Meera** (who asks questions), **Kabir** (who loves maths), and **GrandMa** (who has a story for everything) as they bump into everyday puzzles. Each comic ends with a thinking tool that helps solve real problems.

## 💡 Each Tool Includes

- **A 3-panel comic** — Meera, Kabir or GrandMa face a real problem
- **An analogy** — A simple picture of the idea
- **A true story from history** — Where the tool came from and how it was used
- **Two reading levels** — Yellow box for ages 10–12, blue box for teens
- **Try this** — Two activities to test the tool yourself
- **A game** (where it fits) — Play and see the tool in action

---

## 🎓 How the Book Works

### For ages 10–12
Focus on the **comic** and the **yellow takeaway box**. Do hands-on activities: dice, coins, drawing, role-play. Use question cards as a game.

### For ages 13–17
Read the **blue "going deeper" box**. Apply tools to news, ads, social media and their own choices. Ask them to argue against a tool: when could it mislead you?

### In a classroom
- Play a game in teams on a projector  
- Make a "tool of the week" scoreboard  
- Have students draw their own three-panel comics  
- Run a weekly thinking journal  
- Link tools to subjects (history, maths, science, civics)
